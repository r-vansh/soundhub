Garden Sound Board

Garden Sounds

Each sound for the garden hero, so you can hear them before they go into the component. All of them are generated live in your browser, with 0 KB of audio files. Press a pad a few times: every press comes out slightly different. Everything is tuned to Refreshed: A♭ major, 96 BPM, mellow e-piano notes over soft sub bass, with the top end rolled off for the same warm lo-fi feel. All notes sit in one scale, so any mix sounds good together.

Ambient

Four takes on the slow pad, all on bright major chords in A♭ at 96 BPM. They stay soft and continuous and fade in over a couple of seconds. Press one to play it and press it again to stop. Only one plays at a time, and it keeps going under the garden below.

Live garden

The real garden component with these sounds wired in. Click to plant, hover or swipe to sway, tap a plant, drag to move, and drop at an edge to remove. Watch for birds. The first click turns sound on.

Keys 1 to 9, 0, Q, W, E, R and T play the pads. On a phone, the first tap unlocks sound, the same way it will in the hero.
Swipe sounds are limited to about 8 rustles and 4 twinkles a second, which is how the hero will keep fast swipes from piling up.
